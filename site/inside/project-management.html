Project management

How an engagement runs, and what the plugin knows about it.

The delivery model

Four fixed phases. What happens inside each is chosen per project from a catalogue of approaches, and a set of disciplines is exercised under whichever you choose.

Key Phase: fixed Approach: chosen per project Discipline: exercised under every approach
  • Phases: the four fixed stages of every engagement.
  • Approaches: how a phase is run. Chosen per project, mixable.
  • Disciplines: what must be under control, whatever the approach.
  • Next engagement: Operations loops back to Proposal.

Ask it

The model ships in the plugin, and pm-advisor reads it for you. Ask in plain words in Claude Code.

You askIt answers from
"Which approach fits our Scoping?"The selection guide, with the combinations that carry risk.
"What does the model say about estimation?"That discipline's page. Same for risk, status reporting, change handling, handover, kick-off.
"Who owns what on this engagement?"The roles and cadence layer.
"What happens in Operations?"That phase's page.
  • It recommends; you decide. What the choice depends on, not a verdict.
  • It never writes. Nothing lands in your repo from asking.
  • It calls the model a working draft. In use, not a ratified standard.
  • More skills are planned around the same model, as a pm-* family.
