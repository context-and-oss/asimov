Three layers

Asimov-skills and agents on top: the conversation and the identity, nothing else. Skills in the middle: every method, one family per column. At the bottom, what each skill works on: role skills on your code, artifact skills on your documents, both in your repository; the advisor on the delivery model. Personas have nothing of their own; they read the artifacts the artifact skills write. The same skill works whichever tool runs it, and when you do the step by hand.

The three phases

Design, specification, build. Each phase is one asimov-skill that writes and one that checks, standing on its own skills and its own piece of your repository. One D101 becomes one S101 and its S102s, and those become code.

  • Everything lives in your repository. Asimov-skills write into it; personas and agents read from it. There is no copy inside the plugin to drift.
  • One rule keeps the layers clean. An asimov-skill calls skills and carries no method. A skill reads artifacts and calls no skill. Two levels, never three.
  • Same name in both tools. What you type is /asimov-spec in Claude Code and $asimov-plugin:asimov-spec in Codex; the skill underneath is one file.
  • The frame is not a step. Project management is the engagement around the feature work; the artifacts it needs, a risk log among them, land in the repository later.
