Before anyone signs, stand-ins for the signers have already read it.

  • A persona is a written reader. /asimov-design-review hands it the design; it reads top to bottom as that person and reports where it stopped and why, then lists cuts, not a score.
  • Business personas read the business design. The technical one waits for the technical half.
  • The dashed row is the one that matters. Your real approver, authored by a short interview, stored as a skill in your repository, reading every design from then on.

The skill file a persona lives in is described under General → Persona review skill.
