What it looks like

You answer questions in Claude Code. The answers become a design document, the D101.

⏺ What should the feature do?
> Customers should export their own invoices.
⏺ Who exports them today, and how often?
> Support, on request. A few a day.
⏺ Which of those requests are urgent?
