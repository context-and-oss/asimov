How it works

From description to pull request, with four human decisions on the way.

Key Asimov writes into your repo Asimov reviews or builds A person decides The stop: no technology before the business signs

Lane by lane

  • You describe the feature.
  • Asimov interviews you, writes the business design, and stops. The technical section is declared open in the file. The personas read it as your signers.
  • Business approver signs the what and why. No technology chosen yet.
  • Asimov writes the technical half on a second run.
  • A reviewer, not the author, checks it is gap-free: buildable without asking a question.
  • Asimov writes the product's architecture once, one D100 per area: its parts, how it starts, where each part lives, what was decided. A design names the parts it changes and never restates them.
  • Asimov cuts the design into one plan: one task per sitting, the names between tasks fixed once, every task's brief in the same file, checked by a script before you see it. A part that does not exist yet is built first, exactly as the architecture says.
  • You approve the plan at the cut with one word. The go renders the task specs, one per task, under .asimov/; nothing is written before it, and a plan that changes after your go asks you again. Coding does not start before that.
  • Agents build one task spec each inside asimov-build, reading your conventions by path; per phase Baley reviews the diff against the task specs and Powell runs every done-when, and the phase is committed on the feature branch; nothing is asked between your go and the pull request.
  • A person approves the pull request.

Where a decision says no, the flow goes back one box. People decide four times, and nothing Asimov runs can move any of their decisions.

What lands in your repo

your-product/
├── documentation/D100-<area>.html         the architecture, one per area: parts, start, layout, decisions
├── documentation/features/D101-*.html      every design, rendered, next to the code
├── documentation/conventions/<stack>/      your house style, read by the agents
├── project/                                the project frame: the project info, the risk log
├── .asimov/                                plans, specs and ledgers: working state, never committed
├── .claude/skills/persona-*/               your personas, authored by interview
├── asimov.md                               the marker Asimov owns
└── .github/                                your pull-request rules carry the gates
