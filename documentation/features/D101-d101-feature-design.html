§2Why

A design written mechanism-first anchors the business to what was convenient to build.

  • Chat is not a document. Nobody read the business design as a page before §6 was written.
  • Wrong bar. Unfinished designs were judged gap-free and failed for what they had not done yet.
  • Lost findings. Review output died with the session; settled deviations were re-decided every run.
  • Locked knowledge. How to render a valid D101 lived only inside the interview command.
Audience. Developers authoring or reviewing a D101, and the humans who approve it.

Business design
Principles, the two axes, the pieces, the rules, the decisions.

§4.1Principles

1
Business shape before mechanism. §6 needs a file at Business design first.
2
The stop is a file. Ending the run makes reading the page the only way on.
3
Findings, not verdicts. Tools surface evidence; people decide the bars.
4
Record, don't suppress. A deviation prints every run with a name on it.
5
One home per method. Contract, skill, command — nothing restated.

6
Technical design
Files, one lifecycle, shared shapes, failures, gotchas.

§6.1Platform

  • Prompt files only; a change lands on the next invocation after a plugin update.
  • Review allow-list Read, Glob, Grep, Skill, Write; never Edit. Authoring unrestricted.
  • documentation/features/D101-* is a shared literal in commands, skills, template, .gitignore (hard rule 9).
⚠ Write is a tool grant, not a path grant. The one-path scope is prompt discipline plus a git-diff check.

§6.2The lifecycle

Entry Work Write to disk Hand-off to people
  1. Phases A and B interview, preview §8a, write Business design, end the run.
  2. The author reads the page; /d101-review runs §8a and the business personas, writes the notes.
  3. Phase C offers the notes, works the open-§6 list, previews §8b, writes Full design, deletes the notes.
  4. A second review runs §8b; the non-author reviewer and the approver decide.

§6.8Known constraints & gotchas

The fingerprint is coarse. It misses a hand edit that touches neither chip.
Legacy documents have no phase. They read as Full design until their next write.
Phase C starts from the file. What was agreed in chat but not written is lost, on purpose.
