What lands in your repo

Nothing yet. A risk log and its kin land under Artifacts → Project management when a command produces them.

A risk log, at project/risk-log.html: one page per repo that holds every risk of the engagement, what it costs, who owns it and what changed since last week.

You sayIt does
/asimov-project risk logShows the register, sorted by state and score, and asks whether to mark it reviewed today.
/asimov-project risk log, add …Asks for name, owner, impact and probability, then gives the risk the next number.
/asimov-project risk log, accept RISK-004Records the decision, its reason and who took it. Mitigated and resolved work the same way.
  • Every decision has a name on it. Nothing beyond Active is set without a reason and a person.
  • History only grows. Every change is a dated line with who made it; nothing is deleted.
  • The score ranks; it does not decide. Impact times probability orders the review, nothing more.
  • Not keeping one is a decision too. An opt-out is recorded with its reason, in the register’s place.
  • In Codex: $asimov-plugin:asimov-project risk log.
