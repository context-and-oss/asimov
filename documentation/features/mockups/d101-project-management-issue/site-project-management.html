What lands in your repo

A risk log, at project/risk-log.html: one page per repo that holds every risk of the engagement, who owns it, how likely and how bad it is, and what changed since last week.

You sayIt does
/asimov-project risk logShows the register, sorted by state and score, and asks whether to mark it reviewed today.
/asimov-project risk log, add …Asks for name, owner, impact and probability, then gives the risk the next number.
/asimov-project risk log, accept RISK-004Records the decision, its reason and who took it. Mitigated, occurred and resolved work the same way. When a risk has occurred, it offers an issue-log entry to handle it.
  • Every decision has a name on it. Every change of state carries a reason and the person who made it; an acceptance also names who decided it.
  • History only grows. Every change is a dated line with who made it; nothing is deleted.
  • The score ranks; it does not decide. Impact times probability orders the review, nothing more.
  • Not keeping one is a decision too. An opt-out is recorded with its reason, in the register's place.
  • In Codex: $asimov-plugin:asimov-project risk log.

An issue log, at project/issue-log.html: one page per repo that holds every question the engagement waits on and every action it needs done, outside the product's backlog, in one list by date.

You sayIt does
/asimov-project issue logShows every open question and action, overdue first, and asks whether to mark the log reviewed today.
/asimov-project issue log, question for …Asks who answers it, who chases it and by when, then gives it the next ISSUE number.
/asimov-project issue log, action: …Asks who does it and by when. A meeting to settle a question is an action that points at it.
/asimov-project issue log, ISSUE-007 answeredRecords the answer and who decided it. An action closes with what came of it.
  • Every entry is chaseable. A named person and a date, never a role and never "sometime".
  • The answer, not "resolved". A question closes with what was decided and by whom, so the log is also the engagement's decision trace.
  • One list, most urgent first. Questions and actions together, by date; the page can filter to questions or actions.
  • It follows a conversation, it never replaces one. Ask first; the log is the reminder.
  • In Codex: $asimov-plugin:asimov-project issue log.
